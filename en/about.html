What is AGORA

Public opinion isn't decided by a person — it's the sum of every argument

AGORA is a real-time political debate community modeled on the ancient Greek public square. Nineteen regions ("poleis") compete every day for public opinion through debate.

How it works

1. Post a topic. Anyone can raise up to three debate topics ("threads") a day.

2. Argue for or against it. Other citizens post their own arguments, and everyone weighs in with upvotes, downvotes, and "idiot" reactions to gauge the strength of each point.

3. Regions compete for dominance. The total engagement and upvotes a region's threads and arguments earn decide who controls that polis. Congress votes and declared wars shift the balance of power further.
