What is AGORA PANGAEA

The world lives on the map — conquest is decided by contribution

AGORA PANGAEA is a real-time conquest map covering 250 countries and 6,000+ territories. Liberate territories with contributions, attack adjacent ones to seize control, and leave a note anywhere on the map for others to react to.

How it works

1. Find a territory on the map. Countries split into provinces, which split into districts; zoom out and nearby territories collapse into a single bubble.

2. Liberate it with contributions. Contributing to an unclaimed territory earns points, and crossing the threshold makes you its ruler.

3. Attack from an adjacent territory. You can only launch an attack from a territory you already control that borders the target — the winner takes control.

4. Leave a note on the map. Pick any spot, post a note, and others can comment or react (upvote, downvote, "fool") to it.

What the map shows

Every territory is in one of three states, and a bubble's ring shows the exact mix of states inside it.
